## Style sheet

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: first production STYLE SHEET for Hanyang Defense, an original Joseon cyberpunk top-down pixel-art defense game. This is a reviewable art direction sheet, not a cinematic concept painting and not a final runtime atlas.
Create a coherent landscape board with a large ACTUAL-GAME-SCALE mock scene occupying the left two thirds and a neatly arranged small sprite study panel occupying the right third. No text or labels anywhere.
Scene: straight top-down orthographic Korean Joseon city, a small Gwanghwamun-style tiled gate at the north, stone plaza and clearly legible narrow earthen streets, short walls, low dark blue-gray hanok roofs. Roads remain unoccluded. Small reddish supernatural enemy sprites pour from south and side lanes toward the plaza in readable dense groups. Four distinct defensive structures: wooden wheeled hwacha rocket rack with a few orange projectiles and compact impacts; tall carved jangseung wooden face post physically blocking a narrow lane; squat stone beacon with subtle teal lamp; bronze armillary sensor with a clearly visible ring. One disabled structure extinguished but still physically present. Small teal local connection indicators. No huge magic beams.
Right sprite study panel on flat desaturated charcoal: aligned pairs of active and inactive sprites of the same four structures, and a tiny basic enemy walking sprite pair. Clean separation with generous blank margins. Facility silhouettes chunky and readable at roughly 40x40 logical pixels, enemies roughly 12x16 logical pixels; simple 1-2 pixel outline, 3-tone shading, nearest-neighbor pixel clusters, no microtexture, no antialiasing or painterly details. Larger display only by integer pixel enlargement. Low saturation earth brown, stone gray, wood, dark roof blue; limited teal network, orange fire and red danger accents.
The visual feeling is an intimate top-down pixel village simulation transformed into a large-scale defense battle. Gameplay readability first, modest flat daylight illumination. No isometric diamonds, no dramatic perspective, no UI text, no watermark, no copied commercial game assets, no citywide neon glow, no cinematic smoke. Match all sprite scales and lighting across scene and study panel.

## hwacha

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single isolated original game sprite source for Hanyang Defense. Subject: a Joseon Korean wooden wheeled hwacha rocket launcher, chunky rectangular wooden rocket rack pointing north, two small wooden wheels, muted brown wood and dark iron, a tiny orange ignition strip; idle only, no firing or projectile. STYLE: extremely simple low-resolution pixel art, designed as approximately a 40 by 40 logical pixel sprite enlarged by nearest-neighbor to show pixels. Broad pixel clusters, only 3 shades per material, dark 1-pixel outline, tiny highlights, absolutely no photoreal texture or high resolution painted detail. CAMERA: near top-down orthographic, aligned to a square tile grid with a little front face visible, NOT isometric diamond. Entire object fits centered in the square canvas with generous equal transparent margins, bottom-center ground contact pivot. Transparent background with real alpha, no checkerboard, no labels, no border, no tile or scene. One object only, idle active state. Low-saturation Joseon wood/stone/roof palette, restrained cyberpunk accent. No detached glow particles, no blurry glow, no cast background shadow, no text or watermark. Clear readable silhouette when reduced to 40 pixels.
